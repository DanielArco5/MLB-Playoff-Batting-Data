Skyline Earth

Every postseason offense as a skyscraper in its team's city. The taller the tower, the higher the team's OPS that October.

Colour key: how far the team went

    From far away each city is one wide column. Its height is the median OPS of the campaigns there and its colour is their median finish. It fades as you fly in to the real towers.

    Click a tower to see that team's postseason.

    Postseason streak

    Drag to move, scroll to zoom, hold Ctrl (or the middle button) and drag to tilt. Towers are 150 m wide and stand 250 m apart, oldest season at the centre of each downtown.
